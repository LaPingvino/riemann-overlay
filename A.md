## A2 — tested, and FORCED. Exact coincidence was never load-bearing.

**The test.** Fatten atoms to radius `r`: `Hr r γ := #{ρ : |γ_ρ − γ| ≤ r}`,
with `H = Hr 0` (`Hr_zero`). Rerun the two structural facts. Both survive
**verbatim at every radius**, proved in `Riemann/Basic.lean`, axiom-free:

* `on_line_of_Hr_eq_one` — an `r`-isolated zero is critical, for every `r ≥ 0`;
* `odd_Hr_inhabited` — a cluster of odd mass contains a critical zero, for
  every `r ≥ 0`.

**Why it is forced, in one line:** *the mirror map preserves ordinates exactly*,
so an off-line zero's twin lies at vertical distance `0` and is therefore inside
**every** cluster that contains the zero. Vertical blurring cannot separate a
zero from its reflection.

**The ceiling theorem hiding in the forced-ness** (per protocol): *no amount of
vertical smoothing breaks the gauge orbit.* The entire genre of "resolve the
double-zero/off-line-pair degeneracy by working at finite resolution" is dead,
and it is dead for a reason that has nothing to do with estimates — it is the
geometry of the reflection.

**But A2's second claim stands and is a unification.** The suspicion that the
fattened theory improves constants and dissolves W6's circularity is *correct*,
and the evidence was already in Proposition 2:

> `N⊛ + (2/λ)∫₀^λ 𝒩(α)dα = TL(λ + log(2+λ)/(π²λ) + O(√log(2+λ)/λ))`

The left-hand side **is** a cluster census at scale `λ/L`: `N⊛` counts exact
coincidences, `𝒩` counts near-coincidences weighted by proximity. Our exact
theory is what you get by *discarding* the `𝒩` term (`𝒩 ≥ 0`). So the
Gallagher–Mueller identity natively speaks the fattened language, and

> **A2's fattened theory ≡ [OPEN 0′].**

Keeping `𝒩` instead of throwing it away *is* working at finite resolution.
That is one register's item and another's open problem turning out to be the
