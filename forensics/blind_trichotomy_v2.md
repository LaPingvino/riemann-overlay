## Two claims this read makes that the cut is for

**(1) Our two commonest death classes have antagonistic remedies.**
FUSED wants more coordinates; SLACK wants a tighter container; a tighter
container has fewer coordinates. A program that answers slack by tightening
makes fusion worse, and a program that answers fusion by enriching buys slack.
With 6 FUSED and 3 SLACK on the books, that predicts *oscillation* — and `#10`
is the fingerprint of one full swing: we extended the support and were harmed.
Prediction, falsifiable: future entries will alternate bins rather than
accumulate in one, and any single-direction push will produce its opposite
within two cycles.

**(2) Every FUSED death is a conservation statement wearing a shroud.**
"The mirror preserves ordinates exactly." "`μ_T` has no `β` coordinate."
"Phases shared." "One extremal problem, two faces." None of these is an
obstruction to route around; each *names an invariant*. If the silence problem
is "replace *nothing is there* with *something is conserved*", then the FUSED
bin is already a harvested list of conserved quantities — six of them, obtained
by failing rather than by searching. The register has been asking where to look
for such a quantity while the census manufactured them and filed them as
deaths.

**Might-mean:** the census's most-populated bin is not a list of what stopped
us, it is the project's only completed search for invariants, and it was run
backwards.
